Blackout hits Puerto Rico

Puerto Rico faced an island-wide blackout affecting all 1.4 million power customers during Easter preparations, disrupting tourism, businesses, and transportation. Over 78,000 also lost water access, with restoration expected in 48-72 hours. A transmission system disturbance caused the outage, exacerbating long-standing grid vulnerabilities worsened by Hurricane Maria in 2017. Residents, including vulnerable populations like a mother caring for a child with Down syndrome, struggled without generators. The grid relies heavily on fossil fuels (94%), with slow renewable energy adoption. Political figures criticized Luma Energy and Genera PR, demanding contract cancellations, while officials noted White House outreach. Chronic underinvestment, a 40% poverty rate, and infrastructure neglect persist, with the latest outage marking the first since New Year’s Eve.
