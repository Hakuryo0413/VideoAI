**Article 3 Summary:**  
Camp Century, a covert U.S. military base under Greenland’s ice sheet (1960–1967), was initially part of Project Iceworm, a missile launch plan during the Cold War. Later, it hosted climate research, drilling a historic ice core revealing 100,000 years of climate data. Abandoned due to ice movement, its remnants, including radioactive waste, risk environmental exposure if melting accelerates. Dr. Robert Weiss, a former camp physician, recounted life in the nuclear-powered facility. Scientists warn climate change could destabilize buried waste. The site’s ice core remains pivotal in paleoclimatology, with recent studies uncovering plant fossils indicating past ice-free periods.

**Article 8 Summary:**  
The Alien Enemies Act, last used in WWII, allows deporting nationals from hostile states during war. Trump applied it to Venezuela’s Tren de Aragua, claiming they wage “irregular warfare.” Legal challenges argue the gang isn’t a state entity, making the invocation unlawful. Historically used against wartime enemies, the law bypasses immigration courts, raising due process concerns. Experts note risks of misuse for mass deportations, though courts may reject its application to non-state groups. Past uses targeted Germans, Italians, and Japanese, with lasting civil rights controversies.
