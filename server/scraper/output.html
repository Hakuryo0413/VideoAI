Elon Musk compares himself to Buddha

Elon Musk compared himself to Buddha during a rare interview with mainstream media, reflecting on his role in the Trump administration and the Department of Government Efficiency (DOGE). He acknowledged DOGE’s $160 billion in budget cuts, short of his $1 trillion goal, and admitted some federal employees were mistakenly laid off. Musk, who plans to step back from DOGE, defended his approach, stating achieving deeper cuts requires enduring “complaints.” He shared anecdotes, including late-night ice cream runs with Trump, and joked about his White House office’s security. Despite tensions with media, the session was non-confrontational, with Musk appearing reflective yet resigned about his political challenges.
