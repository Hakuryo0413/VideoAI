North Macedonia nightclub fire

A ‘city under the ice’

Camp Century, a secret U.S. military base built under Greenland’s ice in 1960, aimed to test missile launch sites (Project Iceworm) during the Cold War. Abandoned in 1967, its nuclear reactor and waste remain buried. Scientific research there, including ice core drilling, advanced paleoclimatology, revealing 100,000 years of climate data. Studies warn melting ice could expose hazardous materials by 2100. Dr. Robert Weiss, a former camp physician, shared memories of the subterranean facility. Recent radar mapping and rediscovered sediment samples show Greenland’s ice-free past, raising concerns about sea-level rise. The site symbolizes Cold War geopolitics and climate science milestones.

What’s the Alien Enemies Act and why is Trump invoking it now?

The Alien Enemies Act, enacted in 1798, allows wartime detention/deportation of foreign nationals. Trump invoked it against Venezuela’s Tren de Aragua gang, claiming they wage "irregular warfare." Critics argue the law, historically used during declared wars (e.g., WWII internments), misapplies to non-state actors. A judge temporarily blocked deportations, but 250+ were sent to El Salvador. Legal challenges cite due process violations, as the act bypasses courts. Experts warn it risks targeting marginalized groups, echoing past abuses. While some conservatives support its use for national security, Democrats and advocates call for repeal, citing xenophobic origins and potential for misuse.
